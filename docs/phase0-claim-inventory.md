**Tagging key:**

- **MEASURED** — a specific number/fact directly confirmed against source (repo code, logged pipeline run, or shipped config) in `xylo`/`unawain`.
- **OBSERVED** — true and consistent with the source repos, but not a single precise logged number (e.g. a qualitative architecture fact, or a value confirmed by pattern/logic rather than an exact constant).
- **INFERRED** — plausible and probably directionally true, but not directly confirmed against source in this pass — needs your (Ralph's) confirmation.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — asserted with no supporting evidence found anywhere; on the site by assumption/habit, not by evidence.
- **REMOVE** — should not migrate as-is (fabricated visual, unverifiable marketing term, or asserts something no repo/doc supports).
